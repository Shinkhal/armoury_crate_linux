# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone participating in project spaces is expected to help make them
welcoming and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Discuss technical disagreements calmly and focus on the work.
- Accept that people have different backgrounds, experience, and hardware.
- Respect privacy. Do not post another person's personal information or
  private communications without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, sexualized
attention, doxxing, and sustained disruption of discussions are not acceptable.
Do not use project spaces to promote hateful or demeaning content.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer at
[shinkhalsinha@gmail.com](mailto:shinkhalsinha@gmail.com). Do not report conduct concerns in a public issue.
The maintainer will review reports and may edit or remove content, close
discussions, or restrict participation when needed.

This policy applies to the repository, its issue and pull-request discussions,
and project spaces when someone is representing this project.
